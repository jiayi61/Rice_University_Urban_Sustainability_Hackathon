# Boston Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Gillette Stadium in Foxborough, Massachusetts. The host market is Boston, but venue analysis should include Foxborough, Norfolk County, regional transit and roads, and statewide Massachusetts data.

## Municipal and Regional Repositories

- [Analyze Boston](https://data.boston.gov/): City of Boston's open data portal for 311, permits, facilities, public safety, transportation, neighborhoods, and civic datasets.
- [Boston GIS Open Data](https://bostonopendata-boston.opendata.arcgis.com/): spatial layers for parcels, streets, planning, landmarks, parks, schools, and administrative boundaries.
- [MAPC DataCommon](https://datacommon.mapc.org/): Metropolitan Area Planning Council regional data for land use, demographics, housing, environment, and transportation.

## State-Level Repositories

- [MassGIS](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massgis-bureau-of-geographic-information): Massachusetts Bureau of Geographic Information source for statewide parcels, roads, imagery, land use, hydrography, boundaries, and environmental data.
- [MassDOT Open Data Portal](https://geo-massdot.opendata.arcgis.com/): state transportation layers including roads, bridges, traffic counts, crashes, projects, and transit-related datasets.

## Transit and Mobility

- [MBTA Developer Portal](https://www.mbta.com/developers): GTFS, real-time feeds, APIs, alerts, and schedule data for regional transit analysis.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Venue access: commuter rail, shuttle routes, park-and-ride lots, highway network, pedestrian paths near Foxborough.
- Visitor movement: Boston hotel clusters, airport connections, intercity rail, regional bus terminals.
- Safety and resilience: flood zones, heat indicators, emergency facilities, hospital access.
- Regional equity: ACS demographics, transit-dependent populations, travel-time analysis.
