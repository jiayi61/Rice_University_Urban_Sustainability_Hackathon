# Seattle Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Lumen Field in Seattle, Washington. Seattle's venue sits near downtown, regional rail, ferry, bus, and major event corridors, so city, county, regional, transit, and Washington statewide sources all matter.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [Seattle Open Data](https://data.seattle.gov/): city datasets for 311, permits, public safety, transportation, public services, parks, and operations.
- [Seattle GeoData](https://data-seattlecitygis.opendata.arcgis.com/): GIS layers for parcels, streets, boundaries, public works, trees, facilities, and planning.
- [King County GIS Open Data](https://gis-kingcounty.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide parcels, boundaries, transportation, public health, environment, facilities, and regional layers.

## Regional Repositories

- [Puget Sound Regional Council Data](https://www.psrc.org/our-work/data): regional datasets for transportation, land use, demographics, housing, economy, and planning.

## State-Level Repositories

- [Washington Geospatial Open Data Portal](https://geo.wa.gov/): statewide GIS data for boundaries, environment, transportation, imagery, parcels, and public services.
- [WSDOT Data](https://wsdot.wa.gov/about/data/): state transportation data, traffic, projects, travel times, bridges, and roadway assets.

## Transit and Mobility

- [Sound Transit Open Transit Data](https://www.soundtransit.org/help-contacts/business-information/open-transit-data-otd): GTFS and service data for regional rail and bus service.
- [King County Metro Mobile and Web Apps](https://kingcounty.gov/en/dept/metro/rider-tools/mobile-and-web-apps): app and data resources for Metro service information.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: Link light rail, Sounder, bus, ferry connections, pedestrian routes, event street closures, parking.
- Visitor movement: airport light rail access, downtown hotels, waterfront, intercity rail, regional transit transfers.
- Public realm: sidewalks, bike lanes, street trees, public spaces, ADA access, crowd corridors.
- Risk and resilience: heat, wildfire smoke, earthquake/liquefaction zones, flood, hospitals, emergency facilities.
