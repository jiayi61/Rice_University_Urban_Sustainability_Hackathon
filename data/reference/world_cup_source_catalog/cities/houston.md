# Houston Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: NRG Stadium in Houston, Texas. Houston has strong municipal, county, appraisal district, and regional planning repositories. 
Thank you to Matt Drewensky from the Fondren GIS Data Center with supplying these links.

## Rice U GIS/Data Center
The Rice U GIS/Data Center hosts large datasets for researchers in the Rice community, including regional high-resolution aerial photography, LiDAR point clouds and DEMs, and spatial databases of addresses, customers, and businesses. This data can be accessed from our GIS Lab in B40 in the basement of Fondren Library.

## Municipal Repositories

- [City of Houston Open Data Portal](https://data.houstontx.gov/): general city data including 311, public safety, permits, public works, and civic datasets.
- [MyCity GIS Open Data Portal](https://houston-mycity.opendata.arcgis.com/): city GIS layers for boundaries, planning, transportation, parks, districts, and public services.
- [Houston Public Works Open Data Hub](https://geohub.houstontx.gov/): infrastructure data including traffic counts, utilities, drainage, sidewalks, permits, and public works assets.

## County-Level Repositories

- [Harris County GIS Open Data Portal](https://geo-harriscounty.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide GIS layers for boundaries, facilities, precincts, districts, and service geography.
- [Harris Central Appraisal District GIS Downloads](https://hcad.org/pdata/pdata-gis-downloads.html): parcel, appraisal, ownership, and property-value spatial data.
- [Harris County Dashboards and Datasets Hub](https://www.harriscountytx.gov/Services-Portal/Dashboards-Datasets-Hub): county dashboards and datasets for public services, air quality, and service locators.

## Regional Repositories

- [H-GAC Regional GIS Data Hub](https://gishub-h-gac.hub.arcgis.com/): Houston-Galveston Area Council regional datasets for transportation, environment, land use, and geodetic resources.
- [H-GAC Research and Data](https://www.h-gac.com/research): regional planning research, transportation, water quality, and analytical tools.

## State-Level Repositories

- [Texas Geographic Information Office DataHub](https://data.geographic.texas.gov/): statewide parcels, address points, imagery, elevation, hydrography, and boundaries.
- [TxDOT Open Data Portal](https://gis-txdot.opendata.arcgis.com/): state roads, traffic counts, projects, crashes, bridges, and transportation infrastructure.
- [Texas Water Development Board GIS Data](https://www.twdb.texas.gov/mapping/gisdata.asp): aquifers, river basins, water planning, flood, and water infrastructure layers.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: light rail stations, bus stops, pedestrian routes, road closures, parking, traffic counts.
- Flood and heat: flood hazard, drainage, bayous, impervious surface, tree canopy, cooling centers.
- Public health: hospitals, clinics, air monitoring, vulnerable populations, environmental facilities.
- Regional travel: H-GAC travel-demand, commuter flows, airport connections, freeway performance.
