# Atlanta Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, Georgia. For World Cup analysis, use City of Atlanta sources for local conditions, Fulton County for parcels and county geography, Atlanta Regional Commission for regional planning, and Georgia statewide repositories for transportation and base layers.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [City of Atlanta GIS Open Data](https://gisdata-atlanta.opendata.arcgis.com/): local GIS layers for boundaries, planning, transportation, parks, public safety, public works, and city facilities.
- [Fulton County GIS Open Data](https://gis-fultoncountyga.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide administrative boundaries, parcels, voting geography, public facilities, and other GIS layers.

## Regional Repositories

- [Atlanta Regional Commission Open Data](https://opendata.atlantaregional.com/): regional land use, transportation, population, employment, water, environmental, and planning data for metro Atlanta.

## State-Level Repositories

- [Georgia Geospatial Data Hub](https://data.georgiaspatial.org/): statewide clearinghouse for boundaries, imagery, elevation, transportation, environment, and administrative layers.
- [Georgia Department of Transportation Open Data](https://georgia-department-of-transportation-gdot.hub.arcgis.com/): roads, traffic counts, projects, crashes, bridges, and other statewide transportation layers.

## Transit and Mobility

- [MARTA Developer Resources](https://itsmarta.com/app-developer-resources.aspx): rail and bus schedule feeds, real-time feeds, and developer resources for Atlanta transit analysis.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: MARTA stations, bus stops, pedestrian network, road closures, parking, curb management.
- Heat and exposure: tree canopy, impervious surface, land cover, cooling centers, public facilities.
- Equity and access: Census ACS tables, transit frequency, travel time, disability access, language access.
- Regional movement: ARC travel-demand layers, commuter flows, major event corridors.
