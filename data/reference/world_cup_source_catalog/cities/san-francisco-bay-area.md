# San Francisco Bay Area Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Levi's Stadium in Santa Clara, California. The host market is the San Francisco Bay Area, while the venue geography sits in Santa Clara County near San Jose. This guide includes San Francisco, Santa Clara, San Jose, MTC, transit operators, and California statewide sources.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [DataSF](https://datasf.org/opendata/): San Francisco's open data program and catalog for city services, transportation, permits, public safety, environment, and public works.
- [San Francisco Open Data](https://data.sfgov.org/): Socrata portal for San Francisco datasets.
- [Santa Clara County GIS Open Data](https://data-sccgov.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide GIS data for parcels, boundaries, facilities, transportation, planning, and public services.
- [Santa Clara Open Data](https://data-santaclara.opendata.arcgis.com/): City of Santa Clara GIS layers for the venue municipality.
- [San Jose Open Data](https://data.sanjoseca.gov/): civic datasets for San Jose, the largest city near the venue.
- [San Jose GIS Open Data](https://gisdata-csj.opendata.arcgis.com/): San Jose GIS layers for streets, planning, boundaries, public works, and services.

## Regional Repositories

- [Metropolitan Transportation Commission Open Data](https://opendata.mtc.ca.gov/): Bay Area regional transportation, transit, travel, boundaries, and planning datasets.

## State-Level Repositories

- [California State Geoportal](https://gis.data.ca.gov/): statewide GIS data for boundaries, environment, hazards, imagery, transportation, and public services.
- [Caltrans GIS Data](https://gisdata-caltrans.opendata.arcgis.com/): highways, traffic, crashes, bridges, projects, and transportation assets.

## Transit and Mobility

- [VTA Open Data](https://data.vta.org/): Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority route, service, and transit datasets.
- [BART GTFS](https://www.bart.gov/schedules/developers/gtfs): BART schedule and transit feed resources.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: VTA light rail, Caltrain, BART connections, shuttle zones, pedestrian routes, parking, regional highways.
- Regional travel: SFO, SJC, and OAK airport access; hotel clusters; intercity rail; freeway bottlenecks.
- Heat and resilience: tree canopy, impervious surface, wildfire smoke, flood, sea-level rise, cooling centers.
- Equity and access: transit frequency, language access, disability access, ACS demographics, worker travel patterns.
