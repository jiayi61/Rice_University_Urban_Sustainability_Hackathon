# Miami Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, Florida. Venue and visitor analysis should prioritize Miami-Dade County, Miami Gardens, City of Miami, South Florida regional data, and Florida statewide repositories.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [Miami-Dade County GIS Open Data](https://gis-mdc.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide GIS layers for parcels, boundaries, transit, public facilities, environmental data, public safety, and infrastructure.
- [Miami Gardens ArcGIS Organization](https://miamigardens.maps.arcgis.com/home/index.html): maps and GIS resources for the venue municipality.
- [City of Miami GIS Open Data](https://miami-gis-open-data-miami.hub.arcgis.com/): city GIS layers for planning, boundaries, public services, public works, and urban infrastructure.
- [City of Miami ArcGIS Organization](https://cityofmiami.maps.arcgis.com/home/index.html): maps and web applications for City of Miami data and services.

## Regional Repositories

- [South Florida Regional Planning Council GIS Hub](https://gis-sfrpc.opendata.arcgis.com/): regional planning, resilience, environmental, and transportation data for South Florida.

## State-Level Repositories

- [Florida GeoPortal](https://geodata.myflorida.com/): statewide geospatial data for boundaries, environment, transportation, imagery, hazards, and public services.
- [Florida Geographic Data Library](https://www.fgdl.org/): statewide GIS downloads maintained through the University of Florida GeoPlan Center.
- [FDOT Open Data Hub](https://gis-fdot.opendata.arcgis.com/): roadway, traffic, crash, bridge, project, and transportation asset data.

## Coastal and Hazard Data

- [NOAA Digital Coast](https://coast.noaa.gov/digitalcoast/data/home.html): coastal elevation, shoreline, land cover, sea-level rise, and hazard datasets.
- [FEMA National Flood Hazard Layer](https://www.fema.gov/flood-maps/national-flood-hazard-layer): flood hazard layers for local screening and planning.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: Miami-Dade transit routes, parking, road network, pedestrian routes, rideshare zones, airport connections.
- Flood and heat: sea-level rise, storm surge, flood zones, canopy, impervious surface, cooling centers.
- Public health: hospitals, clinics, heat vulnerability, air quality, emergency facilities.
- Visitor analysis: hotel geography, beach/downtown access, regional travel between Miami, Fort Lauderdale, and Miami Gardens.
