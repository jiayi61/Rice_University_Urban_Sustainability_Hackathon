# Los Angeles Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. The host market spans Los Angeles city, Inglewood, Los Angeles County, SCAG, LA Metro, and California statewide sources.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [City of Los Angeles Open Data](https://data.lacity.org/): city datasets for 311, permits, public safety, transportation, services, and civic operations.
- [Los Angeles GeoHub](https://geohub.lacity.org/): city GIS layers for boundaries, parcels, planning, streets, public works, and neighborhood geography.
- [Los Angeles County Enterprise GIS Hub](https://egis-lacounty.hub.arcgis.com/): countywide spatial layers, maps, and GIS services.
- [Los Angeles County Open Data](https://data.lacounty.gov/): county datasets for health, public safety, services, parcels, transportation, and operations.

## Regional Repositories

- [SCAG GIS Open Data](https://gisdata-scag.opendata.arcgis.com/): Southern California Association of Governments regional data for land use, transportation, demographics, housing, environmental justice, and planning.

## State-Level Repositories

- [California State Geoportal](https://gis.data.ca.gov/): statewide GIS data for boundaries, environment, hazards, imagery, transportation, and public services.
- [Caltrans GIS Data](https://gisdata-caltrans.opendata.arcgis.com/): state highway network, traffic, projects, crashes, bridges, and transportation assets.

## Transit and Mobility

- [LA Metro Developer Resources](https://developer.metro.net/): GTFS, APIs, route, schedule, and real-time transit resources.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: Metro rail and bus connections, shuttle zones, parking, curb space, pedestrian routes, major corridors.
- Visitor movement: airport access from LAX, hotel clusters, fan zones, intercity rail, rideshare pickup areas.
- Heat and air: urban heat, tree canopy, air quality, wildfire smoke exposure, cooling centers.
- Equity and access: transit frequency, travel time, language access, disability access, environmental justice indicators.
