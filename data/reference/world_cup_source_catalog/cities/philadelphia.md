# Philadelphia Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. Philadelphia has a strong local open-data ecosystem, with regional support from DVRPC and statewide support from PASDA and PennDOT.

## Municipal Repositories

- [OpenDataPhilly](https://opendataphilly.org/): civic data catalog for Philadelphia and the region, including public services, transportation, property, environment, and community datasets.
- [Philadelphia CityGeo Open Data](https://citygeo-phl.opendata.arcgis.com/): official city GIS layers for boundaries, parcels, streets, planning, public works, parks, and public facilities.

## Regional Repositories

- [DVRPC Data](https://www.dvrpc.org/data/): Delaware Valley Regional Planning Commission data for transportation, land use, demographics, travel modeling, freight, environment, and regional planning.

## State-Level Repositories

- [PASDA](https://www.pasda.psu.edu/): Pennsylvania Spatial Data Access, the statewide GIS clearinghouse for imagery, parcels, elevation, hydrography, environment, and administrative layers.
- [PennDOT OneMap](https://gis.penndot.gov/onemap/): state transportation mapping and infrastructure data.
- [PennShare Open Data](https://data-pennshare.opendata.arcgis.com/): Pennsylvania state agency open geospatial datasets.

## Transit and Mobility

- [SEPTA Developer Resources](https://www.septa.org/developer/): GTFS, real-time transit feeds, APIs, and schedule data.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium district: Sports Complex access, Broad Street Line, bus routes, parking, pedestrian routes, event closures.
- Visitor movement: airport and Amtrak access, hotel clusters, Center City to stadium travel, fan festival geography.
- Public realm: sidewalks, bike lanes, trees, shade, public spaces, ADA access.
- Risk and resilience: flooding, heat, hospitals, emergency services, air quality, environmental facilities.
