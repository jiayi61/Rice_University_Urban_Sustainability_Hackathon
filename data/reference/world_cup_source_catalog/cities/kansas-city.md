# Kansas City Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: Arrowhead Stadium in Kansas City, Missouri. The host region crosses the Missouri-Kansas state line, so this guide includes KCMO, Jackson County, the Mid-America Regional Council, Missouri statewide data, and Kansas statewide data.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [KCMO Open Data](https://data.kcmo.org/): city datasets for 311, permits, code cases, public safety, neighborhoods, and public services.
- [KCMO GIS Open Data](https://data-kcmo.opendata.arcgis.com/): spatial layers for boundaries, parcels, public works, transportation, parks, and administrative geography.
- [Jackson County GIS Open Data](https://data-jacksongov.opendata.arcgis.com/): county GIS data for parcels, boundaries, facilities, transportation, and election geography.

## Regional Repositories

- [Mid-America Regional Council GIS Open Data](https://data-marc.opendata.arcgis.com/): regional transportation, land use, environment, demographics, and planning layers for the Kansas City metro area.

## State-Level Repositories

- [MoDOT Open Data Portal](https://data-modot.opendata.arcgis.com/): Missouri roadway, bridge, traffic, safety, project, and transportation asset data.
- [Missouri Spatial Data Information Service](https://msdis.missouri.edu/): statewide Missouri GIS clearinghouse for boundaries, imagery, elevation, hydrography, and other base layers.
- [Kansas Data Access and Support Center](https://www.kansasgis.org/): Kansas statewide GIS clearinghouse for boundaries, parcels, imagery, transportation, elevation, and environmental data.
- [Kansas DOT KART](https://kart.ksdot.gov/): Kansas transportation maps, roadway data, traffic, and asset layers.

## Transit and Mobility

- [RideKC](https://ridekc.org/): regional transit service information for buses, streetcar, microtransit, and rider resources.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: Truman Sports Complex roads, parking, shuttle corridors, bus routes, pedestrian paths.
- Regional travel: cross-state commute flows, interstate corridors, airport access, incident-prone routes.
- Heat and environment: tree canopy, impervious surfaces, floodplain, air quality, public cooling facilities.
- Equity and access: ACS demographics, car access, transit access, disability access, language access.
