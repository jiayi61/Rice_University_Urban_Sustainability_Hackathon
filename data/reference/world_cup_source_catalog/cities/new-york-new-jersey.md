# New York/New Jersey Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. The host market spans New York City, northern New Jersey, Bergen County, the Meadowlands, NJ TRANSIT, MTA, and multi-state regional planning sources.

## City, County, and State Repositories

- [NYC Open Data](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/): city datasets for 311, transportation, housing, public safety, health, facilities, and services.
- [NYC Planning Open Data](https://www.nyc.gov/site/planning/data-maps/open-data.page): administrative boundaries, land use, zoning, PLUTO, MapPLUTO, facilities, and planning geography.
- [New Jersey Geographic Information Network](https://njgin.nj.gov/): statewide New Jersey GIS clearinghouse for boundaries, parcels, imagery, elevation, environment, and public services.
- [Bergen County GIS Open Data](https://gis-bergencountynj.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide GIS layers for the stadium county, including parcels, boundaries, roads, facilities, and planning layers.
- [New Jersey Transportation Open Data](https://njogis-newjersey.opendata.arcgis.com/pages/transportation): state transportation layers through NJ Office of GIS.
- [New York State GIS Clearinghouse](https://gis.ny.gov/): statewide New York GIS datasets, services, imagery, and mapping resources.

## Regional Repositories

- [NYMTC Data and Modeling](https://www.nymtc.org/DATA-AND-MODELING): New York Metropolitan Transportation Council data, forecasts, modeling, and regional transportation resources.
- [Meadowlands Chamber](https://meadowlands.org/): regional context and visitor/business information for the Meadowlands area.

## Transit and Mobility

- [NJ TRANSIT Developer Portal](https://developer.njtransit.com/registration/login): developer access for NJ TRANSIT data and APIs.
- [MTA Developer Resources](https://new.mta.info/developers): New York subway, bus, commuter rail, bridge, tunnel, and real-time data resources.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: NJ TRANSIT rail/bus, Meadowlands shuttle operations, Secaucus transfer, parking, pedestrian routes.
- Regional travel: NYC hotel clusters, PATH, Amtrak, airports, regional highway and transit corridors.
- Crowd movement: station capacity context, street closures, fan-zone geography, bridge and tunnel access.
- Resilience: floodplain, heat, coastal storm surge, air quality, environmental facilities.
