# Dallas Spatial Data Sources

Host geography: AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas. The host market is Dallas, but the venue sits in Arlington, so useful analysis often spans Dallas, Arlington, Tarrant County, Dallas County, and the North Central Texas region.

## Municipal and County Repositories

- [Dallas Open Data](https://www.dallasopendata.com/): city datasets for 311, permits, public safety, infrastructure, code, neighborhoods, and public services.
- [City of Dallas GIS Services](https://gis.dallascityhall.com/): web maps and GIS services for Dallas boundaries, parcels, zoning, public works, and planning layers.
- [City of Arlington Open Data](https://data-arlingtontx.opendata.arcgis.com/): GIS datasets for the venue municipality, including boundaries, public facilities, transportation, planning, and infrastructure.
- [Tarrant County GIS Open Data](https://gis-tarrantcounty.opendata.arcgis.com/): countywide boundaries, parcels, roads, election geography, and public facility layers.
- [Dallas County GIS Data](https://www.dallascounty.org/departments/plandev/gis-data.php): Dallas County GIS downloads and mapping resources.

## Regional Repositories

- [NCTCOG Transportation Data](https://www.nctcog.org/trans/data): North Central Texas Council of Governments data for travel modeling, roadway performance, freight, safety, air quality, and regional mobility.

## State-Level Repositories

- [Texas Geographic Information Office DataHub](https://data.geographic.texas.gov/): statewide boundaries, address points, parcels, imagery, elevation, hydrography, and other base layers.
- [TxDOT Open Data Portal](https://gis-txdot.opendata.arcgis.com/): roads, traffic counts, crashes, bridges, projects, and statewide transportation infrastructure.

## Transit and Mobility

- [DART Open Data Information](https://www.dart.org/about/about-dart/open-data-information): schedule and transit datasets for Dallas Area Rapid Transit service.

## Starter Dataset Ideas

- Stadium access: Arlington street network, ridehail and shuttle zones, parking lots, pedestrian routes, regional highways.
- Regional travel: airport access, Dallas-Fort Worth travel corridors, NCTCOG congestion and safety layers.
- Event equity: ACS demographics, car access, transit access, heat and shade, language access.
- Operations context: roadway construction, crash hotspots, emergency facilities, flood-prone corridors.
